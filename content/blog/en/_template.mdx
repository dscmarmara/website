Files whose name starts with `_` are ignored by `getPostSlugs()` in
`src/lib/posts.ts`, so this template never appears on the site. Copy it to
`<slug>.mdx` (same slug in both `en/` and `tr/`) to publish a post.

Frontmatter rules: `author` must be a member slug from `src/data/members.json`,
and `category` must be one of `DEPARTMENTS` in `src/lib/constants.ts` — the
build fails loudly otherwise (see `assertContentIntegrity`). Add `featured: true`
to pin a post to the top of the blog index.
